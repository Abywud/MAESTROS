Maestros Rules

  1. Multiple Squads: Participants may register multiple teams, provided each squad is officially registered with the league.
  2. Registration Fee & Prize Distribution: A fee of KES 400 per team. Payouts: 1st – 50%, 2nd – 25%, 3rd – 15%, 4th – 10%.
  3. Weekly Champion Reward: Each gameweek, the winner receives KES 50 from every other team.
