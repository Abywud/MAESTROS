📜 Maestros Rules

  1. Multiple Squads: Participants may register multiple teams, provided each squad is officially registered with the league.
  2. Registration Fee & Prize Distribution:
    Each team pays KES 400. With 23 teams, the total pool is KES 9,200. Distribution is as follows:
    • 1st place – KES 4,600 (50%)
    • 2nd place – KES 2,300 (25%)
    • 3rd place – KES 1,380 (15%)
    • 4th place – KES 920 (10%)
  3. Weekly Champion Reward: At the end of each gameweek, the champion receives KES 50 from every other team.
  4. Payment Method & Records: After each gameweek, all participants (except the winner) must remit KES 50 to the treasurer. The treasurer will keep proper documentation and deduct KES 100 admin fee weekly.
  5. Tie in Weekly Points: If there is a tie, the reward is shared equally.
  6. Transfers: A maximum of –8 points is allowed for transfers in any gameweek. Free transfers, Free Hit, and Wildcard are permitted as per official FPL rules.
  7. Late Joiners: Teams joining after kickoff must pay KES 500.
  8. Refund Policy: All fees are non-refundable.
  9. Disqualification: Failure to pay for three consecutive gameweeks = disqualification, with no appeal.
  10. Eligibility: Only registered teams are permitted to participate.
  11. Group Engagement: Members must react to group messages for active participation.
