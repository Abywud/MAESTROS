Maestros Rules

  1. Multiple Squads
    Participants may register multiple squads, as long as each is officially registered with the league.
  2. Registration Fee
    A registration fee of KES 400 is required per participant.
    This amount is strictly reserved for awarding the top 4 teams at the end of the season based on final league standings. The prize pool will be distributed as follows:
    • 1st place – 40% of the total pool
    • 2nd place – 30% of the total pool
    • 3rd place – 20% of the total pool
    • 4th place – 10% of the total pool
  3. Weekly Champion Reward
    At the end of each gameweek, the weekly champion shall receive KES 50 from each participant before the start of the next game week.
  4. Payment Method & Records
    The weekly winner shall decide whether payments will be sent directly to them or to the treasurer.
    If sent to the treasurer, they will handle collection, keep official records, and update the group.
    In such cases, the treasurer shall retain KES 100 per gameweek as a service fee.
    If sent directly to the winner, the winner must confirm all receipts with the treasurer for record-keeping.
  5. Points Tie
    In the event of a tie in gameweek points, the winning amount will be shared equally among the tied participants, regardless of other factors.
  6. Transfers
    A maximum of –8 points is allowed in transfers per gameweek.
    Free transfers, Free Hit, and Wildcard are permitted as per official game rules.
  7. Late Joiners
    New participants joining after the league has started must pay a registration fee of KES 500.
  8. Refund Policy
    Registration fees are non-refundable under any circumstances.
  9. Disqualification Clause
    Failure to remit payments on time to three consecutive weekly champions will result in disqualification from the league.
    There is no appeal once disqualified.
  10. Eligibility
    Only registered teams are allowed to participate in the league.
  11. Group Engagement
    Every member is expected to at least react to messages in the group to promote active engagement and communication.
