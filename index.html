Maestros Rules

  1. Multiple Squads
    Participants may register multiple teams, provided each squad is officially registered with the league.
  2. Registration Fee & Prize Distribution
    A registration fee of KES 400 is required per team. This amount is pooled and awarded to the top four teams at the end of the season as follows:
    • 1st place – 50% of the total pool
    • 2nd place – 25% of the total pool
    • 3rd place – 15% of the total pool
    • 4th place – 10% of the total pool
    Pay your Registration (KES 400)
    Recipient: +254706382616
    Open WhatsApp Call Treasurer
    Tip: On mobile, open WhatsApp to coordinate M-Pesa payment and send confirmation.
  3. Weekly Champion Reward
    At the end of each gameweek, the weekly champion shall be entitled to a reward of KES 50 from every other team.
  4. Payment Method & Records
    After each gameweek, all participants, excluding the winner, must remit KES 50 to the treasurer. The treasurer shall ensure proper documentation of all payments and release the total winnings to the weekly champion prior to the commencement of the next gameweek. From the total amount collected each gameweek, the treasurer shall retain KES 100 as an administrative fee to cover record-keeping and payment processing.
  5. Tie in Weekly Points
    In the event of a tie in gameweek points, the weekly reward will be shared equally among the tied Teams.
  6. Transfers
    A maximum of –8 points is allowed for transfers in any gameweek. Free transfers, Free Hit, and Wildcard are permitted in line with the official game rules.
  7. Late Joiners
    Teams joining after the league has commenced must pay a registration fee of KES 500.
  8. Refund Policy
    All registration fees are non-refundable under any circumstances.
  9. Disqualification
    Failure to remit payments on time for three consecutive gameweeks will result in disqualification from the league. No appeals will be entertained once a team is disqualified.
  10. Eligibility
    Only registered teams are permitted to participate in the league.
  11. Group Engagement
    All members are expected to react to messages in the group to encourage active participation and communication.
