Maestros Rules

  1. Multiple Squads
    Participants may register multiple squads, provided each squad is officially registered with the league.
  2. Registration Fee & Prize Distribution
    All participants have paid a registration fee of KES 400 each.
    Number of paid participants: 23.
    Total collected: KES 9,200.
    The pool will be distributed to the top 4 with percentages & amounts shown:
    • 1st place — 40% = KES 3,680
    • 2nd place — 30% = KES 2,760
    • 3rd place — 20% = KES 1,840
    • 4th place — 10% = KES 920
    Pay your Registration (KES 400)
    Recipient (M-Pesa / WhatsApp): +254706382616
    Open WhatsApp Call Treasurer
    Tip: On mobile use WhatsApp to coordinate M-Pesa payment and then confirm via the group.
  3. Weekly Champion Reward
    At the end of each gameweek, the weekly champion shall receive KES 50 from each participant.
  4. Payment Method & Records
    The weekly winner may have payments sent directly or to the treasurer. If sent to the treasurer, the treasurer retains KES 100 per gameweek for record-keeping/processing.
  5. Points Tie
    In the event of a tie in gameweek points the weekly reward shall be shared equally among tied participants.
  6. Transfers
    Maximum transfer penalty of –8 points/GW. Free transfers, Free Hit, and Wildcard allowed per official rules.
  7. Late Joiners
    Teams joining after the league started pay KES 500 registration fee.
  8. Refund Policy
    All registration fees are non-refundable.
  9. Disqualification
    Failure to remit payments to three consecutive weekly champions results in disqualification — no appeal.
  10. Eligibility
    Only registered teams are allowed to participate.
  11. Group Engagement
    All members should react/engage in the group to promote activity.
