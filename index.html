📜 Maestros Rules

  1. Multiple Squads: Participants may register multiple teams, provided each squad is officially registered with the league.
  2. Registration Fee & Prize Distribution:
    Each team pays KES 400. Distribution of the contributed fee is as follows:
    • 1st place – (50%)
    • 2nd place – (25%)
    • 3rd place – (15%)
    • 4th place – (10%)
  3. Weekly Champion Reward & Treasurer Retention:
    At the end of each gameweek, the gameweek winner receives KES 50 from every other team. The treasurer retains KES 100 from each team’s contribution for administrative purposes before distributing the weekly champion rewards.
    4. 2. Registration & Payment:
    Every participant must pay a registration fee of Ksh 400.
    Payments are to be made via M-Pesa using the details below:

    📱 M-Pesa Number: +254 706 382 616

    👤 Treasurer: George Ikwenyi

    📞 Call Treasurer | 💬 WhatsApp Treasurer

    (Ensure you keep your confirmation message as proof of payment)
  4. Tie in Weekly Points: If there is a tie, the reward is shared equally.
  5. Transfers: A maximum of –8 points is allowed for transfers in any gameweek. Free transfers, Free Hit, and Wildcard are permitted as per official FPL rules.
  6. Late Joiners: Teams joining after kickoff must pay KES 500.
  7. Refund Policy: All fees are non-refundable.
  8. Disqualification: Failure to pay for three consecutive gameweeks = disqualification, with no appeal.
  9. Eligibility: Only registered teams are permitted to participate.
  10. Group Engagement: Members must react to group messages for active participation.
